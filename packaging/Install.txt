Install Redactor

1. Move Redactor.app into your home Applications folder: ~/Applications
2. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
   On macOS 14 or earlier, right-click Redactor.app and choose Open the first time.
   On macOS 15 or later, try to open it, then choose System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
3. A shield icon in the menu bar means Redactor is running. Nothing needs to be closed, because there is no window.
4. Redactor starts again at the next login. Turn off Open at Login in the menu bar before you move the app to the Trash.
5. The pattern list is at:

~/Library/Application Support/Redactor/patterns.json

A clipboard-history app can keep a secret after Redactor removes it from the clipboard. Redactor removes that secret again only when the history app puts it back on the clipboard. Redactor does not delete history databases.
